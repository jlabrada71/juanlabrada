# Why big companies share technical details of their systems?

Companies have a few reasons for doing that:

- One is to generate buzz about how cool the technologies the company uses are and about the capabilities the company have for dealing with technical difficulties.
- This generates trust in their clients, and help clients decide on buying their products.
- This technical documentation helps the company create a knowledge base that is not kept in their technical staff minds, and that helps recall the reasons for technical decisions. 
- Creating this documentation enforces a culture of collaboration, and allows the technical staff to be recognized and feel pride of their work, in addition to improving the communication skills of the technical team.
- All these reasons also encourage skillfull candidates to join the company since it shows the potential for career growth and accomplishment.
